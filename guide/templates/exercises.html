Exercises Guide

These are the main exercises for every major muscle group. These movements are commonly used by athletes and bodybuilders to build strength, muscle mass, and overall fitness. Beginners should focus on proper technique before increasing weight.

Chest Exercises

  • Barbell Bench Press – Focus: Middle chest The most popular compound movement for building overall chest size and strength.
  • Incline Dumbbell Press – Focus: Upper chest Helps develop the upper portion of the chest for a fuller look.
  • Decline Bench Press – Focus: Lower chest Targets the lower chest fibers and improves chest thickness.
  • Chest Dips – Focus: Lower chest and triceps A powerful bodyweight exercise for building strength and muscle.
  • Cable Fly – Focus: Inner chest Isolation exercise that improves chest definition and muscle control.

Back Exercises

  • Pull Ups – Focus: Upper back and lats One of the best exercises for developing a wide back.
  • Lat Pulldown – Focus: Latissimus dorsi (lats) Great beginner alternative to pull ups for building back width.
  • Barbell Row – Focus: Middle back thickness Compound exercise for overall back development.
  • Seated Cable Row – Focus: Mid-back and rear delts Helps improve posture and back strength.
  • Deadlift – Focus: Entire posterior chain Builds massive strength in the back, glutes and legs.

Leg Exercises

  • Barbell Squat – Focus: Quadriceps and glutes One of the most effective exercises for building lower body strength.
  • Leg Press – Focus: Quadriceps Machine movement that helps increase leg size and power.
  • Romanian Deadlift – Focus: Hamstrings and glutes Strengthens the posterior chain and improves leg balance.
  • Leg Curl – Focus: Hamstrings Isolation movement for the back of the legs.
  • Calf Raises – Focus: Calves Essential exercise for building lower leg strength.

Shoulder Exercises

  • Overhead Press – Focus: Front and middle deltoids A compound movement that builds overall shoulder strength.
  • Lateral Raises – Focus: Side deltoids Helps create wider shoulders and improves shoulder shape.
  • Rear Delt Fly – Focus: Rear deltoids Important for shoulder balance and posture.
  • Front Raise – Focus: Front deltoids Strengthens the front portion of the shoulders.
  • Barbell Shrugs – Focus: Trapezius (upper traps) Builds strong traps and upper back thickness.

Arm Exercises

Biceps

  • Barbell Curl – Focus: Overall biceps size Classic exercise for building bigger arms.
  • Dumbbell Curl – Focus: Biceps peak Helps isolate each arm for balanced growth.
  • Hammer Curl – Focus: Brachialis and forearms Adds thickness to the arms and improves grip strength.
  • Concentration Curl – Focus: Biceps peak Isolation exercise for improving biceps definition.

Triceps

  • Close Grip Bench Press – Focus: Overall triceps A compound movement that builds triceps power.
  • Tricep Pushdown – Focus: Lateral head Popular cable exercise for triceps definition.
  • Skull Crushers – Focus: Long head of triceps Helps increase arm size and strength.
  • Overhead Tricep Extension – Focus: Long head Important for full triceps development.
  • Dips – Focus: Triceps and lower chest Bodyweight exercise that builds strong arms.

Abs Exercises

  • Crunches – Focus: Upper abs Basic abdominal exercise for strengthening the core.
  • Leg Raises – Focus: Lower abs Targets the lower abdominal muscles.
  • Plank – Focus: Core stability Strengthens the entire core and improves posture.
  • Hanging Leg Raises – Focus: Lower abs Advanced movement for strong abdominal muscles.
  • Russian Twists – Focus: Obliques Targets the side abdominal muscles.

Always Do Some Cardio after Strength Training as Cardio is Important For Our Heart Health !
