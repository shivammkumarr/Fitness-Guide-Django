Weight Gain Guide

What is Healthy Weight Gain?

Healthy weight gain means increasing body weight in a balanced way by gaining muscle mass and improving overall strength. Many people who are underweight want to gain weight to improve their health, strength, and physical appearance.

Healthy weight gain should focus on:

  • Eating enough calories
  • Strength training
  • Consuming nutrient-dense foods
  • Proper recovery and sleep
---

Calorie Surplus for Weight Gain

To gain weight, you must eat more calories than your body burns. This is called a calorie surplus.

Most people can gain weight safely by eating about 300–500 extra calories per day.

Example

  • Maintenance Calories = 2200 kcal
  • Weight Gain Calories = 2200 + 500
  • Daily Target = 2700 kcal

This additional energy helps the body build muscle and increase body weight.

---

High Calorie Foods for Weight Gain

To gain weight effectively, choose foods that provide both calories and nutrients.

Examples of High Calorie Foods

  • Milk
  • Paneer
  • Peanut butter
  • Bananas
  • Rice
  • Potatoes
  • Oats
  • Nuts and seeds
  • Whole wheat bread
  • Dal and legumes
---

Example High Calorie Smoothie

A smoothie is an easy way to increase daily calorie intake.

  • 1 glass milk
  • 30 g oats
  • 1 banana
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon honey

This smoothie provides a good amount of calories, carbohydrates, protein, and healthy fats.

Workout Plan for Weight Gain

Strength training helps ensure that most of the weight gained comes from muscle rather than fat.

Day 1 – Chest

  • Bench Press – 4 sets × 8 reps
  • Incline Dumbbell Press – 3 sets × 10 reps
  • Chest Fly – 3 sets × 12 reps

Day 2 – Back

  • Pull Ups – 4 sets × 8 reps
  • Lat Pulldown – 3 sets × 10 reps
  • Seated Cable Row – 3 sets × 12 reps

Day 3 – Legs

  • Squats – 4 sets × 8 reps
  • Leg Press – 3 sets × 10 reps
  • Leg Curl – 3 sets × 12 reps

Day 4 – Shoulders

  • Shoulder Press – 4 sets × 8 reps
  • Lateral Raises – 3 sets × 12 reps
  • Rear Delt Fly – 3 sets × 12 reps

Day 5 – Arms

  • Bicep Curls – 3 sets × 12 reps
  • Tricep Pushdown – 3 sets × 12 reps

Day 6 – Rest

Day 7 – Rest

Always Do Some Cardio after Strength Training as Cardio is Important For Our Heart Health !

Example Daily Diet for Weight Gain

  • Breakfast: Oats with milk, banana, and peanut butter
  • Snack: Handful of nuts and fruit
  • Lunch: Rice, dal, vegetables, paneer
  • Snack: Peanut butter sandwich and milk
  • Dinner: Roti, vegetables, curd, tofu
  • Before bed: Milk
---

Important Tips for Weight Gain

  • Eat frequently (4–5 meals per day)
  • Choose calorie-dense foods
  • Train with weights regularly
  • Track your progress weekly
  • Sleep 7–9 hours daily

Healthy weight gain should be slow and steady. Most people can gain about 0.25–0.5 kg per week with proper diet and training.
